CONTROLS (H = open this)

ON FOOTW A S D / arrow keys = move · Shift = run · J = jump · Space = punch / shoot · Q = switch weapon
E keyGet in a car (steal it if driven) · pickpocket a person · loot a body · climb out of water · get OUT of your car
IN A CARW = gas · S = brake / reverse · A / D = steer left / right · Space = shoot · E = get out
BOATSame as car. Press E to jump into the water and swim.
PLANEJoystick / W A S D = steer and speed up · at speed hold CLIMB (Shift) to take off · hold LAND (C) to come down on open ground, then E to get out · fly above buildings or you crash (PULL UP! warning)
WATERSwim toward the bank: you climb out by yourself. Or press E.
B / MB = car horn · M = sound on / off
R / NR = reload gun (auto when empty) · N = car radio station
BOMBX or the BOMB button throws a bomb (2 second fuse, big blast: RUN!). You can also pick it with the GUN button and press FIRE. Buy bombs in shops or find them in stockrooms. Bombs are the only thing that can hurt you fast inside a vehicle, and the police throw them at wanted level 4+.
MISSIONSThe arrow and minimap show your mission. Finish missions for money and rank; new kinds unlock as you progress (taxi, kill a target, steal a car, rob a shop). Lose the police by breaking line of sight, swimming, or hiding inside a building.
LIFTEvery building has 3 floors (ground, upper, top). Take the big yellow-railed stairs (two staircases side by side: ground to 1st floor, and 1st floor to the top; the camera swings to the side so you can see the steps), or step on the glowing LIFT pad, stand still for a second (or press E) and the lift takes you to the next floor, then the top, then back down.
HOUSESThe doors are open: just walk into a glowing door (doors are on every side of every block: green = shop, blue = apartment, red = bar) (or press E next to it). Inside, press E at the counter, bed, TV, stash or bar. Walk into the wide green EXIT doorway, or tap the LEAVE button (key L) to go out from any floor. Rob a shop and you get a wanted level when you leave.
VIEWV or the VIEW button = third-person / top view · drag the right side of the screen to look around · T = touch pad on / off
TIMEThe game has a full day and night (10 minutes per day, clock on the minimap). Cheats: day, night, dawn. If it feels slow on your phone, MENU > GRAPHICS lowers the quality (the game also does this by itself).
GG = shadows on / off (the game turns them off by itself if your phone is slow)
CHEATS` (backtick) or the CHEAT button
PHONEDrag left side = move / steer · RUN · JUMP · FIRE/PUNCH · WEAPON · ENTER/EXIT · CHEAT · HELP
TRAPPED BY POLICE?Press E (phone: tap ENTER/EXIT). You jump out on any free side, even at speed. Then hold Shift / RUN and sprint away, cut round buildings, or swim the river: police cars cannot follow into the water. A hint shows on screen when you are trapped.
